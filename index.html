Jingyu Hu

Email: hujingyucd@gmail.com

I am currently a researcher at Tencent Games. I obtained my PhD degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Chi-Wing Fu. Before that, I got the Bachelor’s degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), supervised by Prof. Lin Gao. My research focuses on computer graphics and 3D vision.

If you are interested in my research or potential collaborations, feel free to contact me!
